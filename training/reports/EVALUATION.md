# FaceTilt orientation classifier — evaluation

Generated by `python -m facetilt.train`. Test faces: FairFace **validation** split (10954 images, never used for training), each rendered through the camera simulator and evaluated at all 4 rotations; no-face samples come from held-out scikit-image photos, FairFace validation backgrounds and procedural scenes.

* Parameters: **34,933**
* Model size: fp32 138.5 KB, **int8 46.8 KB**
* Training samples: 403,546
* Best validation accuracy (PyTorch fp32): 94.8%

*Coverage* = share of face frames the app trusts (confidence ≥ 0.7 and not no-face); the rest fall back to gravity (standard auto-rotate). *Confident-wrong* is the harmful case: a face frame trusted with the wrong orientation.
